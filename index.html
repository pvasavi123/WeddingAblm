CHAPTER I • THE MEET-CUTE

The Umbrella & Croissant Heist

"It started with an accidental umbrella theft and a negotiation over the bakery's last almond pastry."

"She had accidentally grabbed my black umbrella from the café umbrella stand. I sprinted 100 meters down Rue Lepic in the drizzling rain calling out 'Excuse me!'. When she turned around, flour on her sweater and the sweetest apologetic giggle, I forgot that I was completely soaked. We went back inside, split the last almond croissant 50/50, and talked until the staff started putting the chairs up."
🥐 Cute Fact: Julian secretly let her have 70% of the croissant because she said she hadn't eaten lunch.
☕ Café de Flore 🌧️ Autumn Rain 🥐 Croissant Peace Treaty
March 6, 2022
CHAPTER II • THE KITCHEN CHAOS

The Great Sunday Fettuccine Catastrophe

"We watched one YouTube tutorial on pasta from scratch and got dangerously overconfident."

"I cracked an egg and the yolk immediately rolled off the flour volcano and splattered on her slippers. Aria retaliated by booping my nose with a handful of semolina flour. Twenty minutes later, we had pasta dough stuck to the cabinet knobs and the kitchen looked like an exploded snowglobe. We ended up sitting on the living room rug eating spicy ramen noodles with extra cheddar cheese, laughing until our ribs ached."
🧀 Cute Fact: Cheesy midnight ramen officially became our celebratory dinner for every anniversary!
🍝 Exploded Flour 🍜 Ramen on the Rug 😂 Pure Giggles
October 12, 2023
CHAPTER III • THE THIRD WHEEL

Campfire Cocoa & Barnaby's Arrival

"We drove 4 hours to stargaze in the pines. We came home with muddy boots and a rescue golden puppy."

"We packed an old pickup truck with string lights, three wool tartan blankets, and two thermoses of hot cocoa with mini marshmallows. At a roadside diner stop in Lake Tahoe, we saw a rescue adoption booth. Aria locked eyes with this chunky little golden retriever with floppy ears. Two hours later, little 'Barnaby' was curled up asleep between us on the tailgate, snoring softly while the Milky Way rotated above."
🐾 Cute Fact: Barnaby wore a custom miniature bow-tie as the official 'Ring Bearer' on our wedding day!
🌌 Stargazing ☕ Marshmallow Cocoa 🐶 Barnaby the Golden
August 18, 2024
CHAPTER IV • THE QUESTION

The Dropped Cue Cards & The 'Yes!'

"He had rehearsed a Shakespearean monologue for three months. It all fell apart in the cutest way possible."

"I had written my whole proposal speech on index cards. As I went down on one knee on the Pacific cliff deck, a sudden gust of ocean wind blew every single card straight off the cliff into the sea! I froze, panicked, and just blurted out: 'Aria, you make my life the most fun adventure, I love how you rescue bees from swimming pools, and I cannot breathe without you. Please marry me!'. She burst into happy tears and tackled me into a bear hug before I could even open the ring box."
💍 Cute Fact: Aria said 'Yes!' so fast that Julian forgot which finger the ring was supposed to go on!
🌊 Big Sur, CA 📜 Escaped Cue Cards 😭 Tears of Joy

The Little Quirks We Love About Each Other

The unglamorous, hilarious, everyday details that make our bond truly ours.

The Clockwise Coffee Rule

Julian prepares Aria's morning latte with exactly 1 pour of oat milk, stirred clockwise 3 times. If stirred counterclockwise, Aria jokingly swears it turns into decaf.

🪴

The Plant Voice Actor

Aria has given dramatic Shakespearean accents and life stories to all 14 houseplants. Julian now unironically says good morning to 'Lord Archibald' the Monstera.

🍕

The Pizza Crust Symbiosis

Julian only eats the cheesy center of pizza slices, while Aria's absolute favorite part is dipping the charred crusts in spicy chili honey. Perfect equilibrium.

🚗

The Unapologetic Car Concerts

On every road trip, Aria is in charge of the playlist and sings every 80s ballad at top volume with enthusiastic hand choreography while Julian harmonizes off-key.
